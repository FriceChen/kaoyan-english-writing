> `01_看材料` 负责“怎么看”；本文件只负责“看懂以后怎么写成英语”。
>
> 图表第一句怎么开始见 `03_Position_Interfaces / O1–O5`；解读“这说明什么”见 `02_Shared_Functional / F2`。
>
> 本文件从具体画面、具体数据句开始，不讲读图方法，不做寓意分析。

- 选哪些信息、哪个是结构、哪个是对比、哪几个数字代表整体 → `01_看材料`。

## 1. 图画描写

### 画面总体

- At the center of the picture, ...
- In the foreground / background, ...
- The scene is set in ...
- The picture is divided into two parts: ...
- On the left / right side of the picture, ...

> 这些是画面内部的继续描述，不是 O1 开场句。

### 人物与动作

- stand beside / in front of ...
- sit at / on ...
- hold ... in one's hand
- point to / look at ...
- walk toward / turn away from ...
- reach for / lean against ...
- bend over / crouch down
- hand ... to ...
- support ... by the arm
- look up / look down at ...

> 只写可见动作；不补人物内心。

### 表情与状态

- look relaxed / satisfied / excited
- look confused / worried / exhausted
- frown / smile / sigh
- with a look of ...
- with sweat dripping down one's face
- out of breath

> 只写可见表情 / 姿态；不虚构心理。

### 场景与物体

- be surrounded by ...
- be filled with ...
- be placed beside / in front of ...
- in the distance
- on the wall / on the desk
- a pile of ... / a stack of ...

> 具体物品名按题目替换；不建“物品词典”。

### 双图 / 前后对比

- A sharp contrast can be seen between ...
- Compared with the first picture, ...
- The second picture shows a clear change in ...
- Both pictures depict ...
- In the first picture, ...; in the second, ...

> 对比 / 变化 / 相似由 `01_看材料` 判断；这里只实现已经判断好的关系。

## 2. 图表与表格

### 数据句入口

- The figure for X stood at Y.
- X reached Y.
- X accounted for Y% of the total.
- The number of X rose / fell from A to B.
- X was the largest / smallest category.

> 图表第一句怎么开始见 `03_Position_Interfaces / O3`；这里从具体数据句开始。

### 上升 / 下降

**上升：**

- rise / increase / grow / climb
- surge / soar（强幅度，慎用）

**下降：**

- fall / decline / decrease / drop
- plunge / plummet（强幅度，慎用）

> 每组保留少量高频词；不追求 10 个同义词。

### 稳定 / 波动

- remain stable / steady
- remain relatively unchanged / remained essentially unchanged (12%)
- remain relatively stable
- level off / plateau
- fluctuate / show some fluctuation
- bar chart / line graph / pie chart / table（图表类型词；chart 为总称）

### 程度 / 速度

**小幅：**

- slightly / marginally / gently

**稳步：**

- steadily / gradually / consistently

**大幅：**

- sharply / dramatically / significantly / substantially

> 三类够用；不把所有程度副词搬进来。

### 占比

- account for ...% of the total
- make up / constitute / represent ...% of ...
- the largest / smallest share
- a quarter / a third / nearly half of the total

### 比较

- higher / lower than ...
- more / less than ...
- compared with / in comparison with ...
- twice as high as ...
- three times as large as ...

### 排名

- rank first / second
- top the list
- be followed by ...
- come next
- record the highest / lowest figure
- remain the largest / smallest category
- stand out from the other categories

> 排序只有在体现明显结构时才写；不机械列 A first, B second, C third。

### 差距

- the gap between A and B widened / narrowed
- the difference remained small
- the two figures moved closer together
- A gradually caught up with B
- A overtook / surpassed B

### 倍数

- twice as high as ...
- three times as large as ...
- double / triple

> 避免 `twice higher than`；倍数用 `twice as high as`。

### 时间变化

- over the period from A to B
- during the same period
- initially / at first
- then / after that
- by the end of the period
- toward the end of the period

### 常见调查维度 / 变量名

图表题面的标题、项目、横纵坐标常用词：

- ways / methods
- reasons / reasons for popularity
- purposes
- frequency of use
- preferences
- daily time spent on ...
- the number of ...
- the proportion / percentage of ...
- the number of applicants
- the number of users / participants
- participation rate / acceptance rate
- market size

### 调查对象与分组

- survey respondents / participants
- age groups / year groups（⚠ 年级分组是类目不是时间：兼职图的"大一→大四"不能当年度变化）
- households / consumers / residents

### 对象属性（题面第二变量）

- consumer acceptance / level of satisfaction
- primary concern / reason for choosing
- preferred activity / preferred mode of transport
- benefits of ...

### 数据口径

- per 100 households（每百户拥有量 ≠ 拥有该物的家庭百分比）
- number of visits / share of ... / volume of ...
- multiple responses were allowed（仅题目确实为多选时；多选占比不能当互斥构成）

### 图表组织（排序与比较）

- ranked first / the largest share / the most frequently selected option
- a rise in ... / a decline in ...
- remained relatively stable / differed across age groups

> `changes in the number of ...` 由上面的上升 / 下降系统实现，不重复堆叠。
> 比例统一写 the proportion / percentage of ...（如 the proportion of respondents choosing flexible employment），或 the employment rate；不要把"就业"和"比例"两个词直接拼成一个名词短语。

### 主题化安全框架（图表 / 一画一表 → 第二段主题）

- the growing popularity of + N
- the increasing use of + N
- the development of + N
- people's growing interest in + N
- people's increasing acceptance of + N
- the growing importance of + N
- the growing number of + 复数 N

> 图表给了明确对象时，优先围绕对象写"流行 / 增长 / 普及 / 接受度"，不要强行拔高成宏大价值观。

## 3. 数字准确性

### from / to / by

- rose from 20% to 30%（起点 20，终点 30）
- rose by 10 percentage points（增加 10 个百分点）
- stood at / reached 30%

> 不要把 `from A to B` 和 `by` 混用；`by` = 差值。

### percentage / percentage points

- 20% → 30%：`an increase of 10 percentage points`
- 不写 `an increase of 10 percent`
- 如写 percent increase，20% → 30% 是 relative increase of 50%

> 考场优先用 `percentage points`，避免歧义。

### approximately / nearly / more than

- about / around / approximately / roughly
- nearly / almost
- just over / just under
- more than / less than

> 图上读不出精确值时，不硬写精确数字。

### respectively

- The figures for A and B were 30% and 20%, respectively.

> 只在顺序明确时用；不滥用。

## 4. 复杂趋势

### 先升后降

- rose initially before falling
- climbed to a peak before dropping
- increased at first and then declined

### 先降后升

- declined at first and then recovered
- fell initially before rebounding
- hit a low before rising again

### 波动

- fluctuated over the period
- showed some fluctuation
- fluctuated before stabilizing

### 趋同 / 分化

- the two figures moved closer together
- the gap narrowed
- A gradually caught up with B
- A and B followed increasingly different paths
- the gap continued to widen
- A grew much faster than B

### 总趋势 + 例外

- Overall, X increased, despite a temporary decline in Y.
- Most categories increased, with the exception of X.
- The general trend was upward, but growth was uneven across categories.
- While all three indicators rose, X increased far more rapidly than the others.

### 结构变化

- the composition shifted toward X
- the share of X expanded at the expense of Y
- X became the largest category
- the dominant category shifted from X to Y
- the structure became more balanced

## 5. 图画 + 图表

> 关系判断（支持 / 补充 / 对照 / 并列）由 `01_看材料` 决定；这里只提供语言。

### 并列

- The picture offers a concrete scene, while the chart supplies the numbers behind it.
- The picture and the chart approach the same issue from different angles.

### 补充

- The chart adds numerical context to the scene shown in the picture.
- The figures provide a broader context for understanding the picture.

### 支持

- The figures are consistent with the pattern shown in the picture.
- The chart offers quantitative support for what the picture suggests.

### 对照

- The chart presents a different pattern from that suggested by the picture.
- The figures do not move in the same direction as the scene in the picture.

> 不默认 `the chart proves the picture`；只有 01 判断为支持时才用支持类表达。

## 6. 三句压缩法

这是表达压缩，不是读图流程。信息选择已经由 `01_看材料` 完成。

```text
Sentence 1: overall pattern
Sentence 2: key contrast
Sentence 3: representative figures
```

示例骨架：

```text
Overall, X ...
The most notable contrast was between A and B: ...
A ..., while B ...
```

> 三句分别对应总趋势、关键对比、代表数字；具体选哪些由 `01_看材料` 已定。

---

## 7. 不要做什么

- 不逐项罗列所有数字。
- 不把每个类别都写一遍。
- 不虚构精确值；读不出就用 approximately / nearly / just over。
- 不默认 `the chart proves the picture`。
- 不写 `twice higher than`；用 `twice as high as`。
- 不把 percentage points 写成 percent。
- 不把 fastest growth 写成 largest category。
- 不在本文件讲“先看单位 / 找结构 / 选代表数字”；这些归 `01_看材料`。
- 不在本文件做“这说明什么”的解读；这些归 `F2`。
