# 小作文

> 小作文 = 交际任务，不是议论文。读题 → 定格式 → 定功能 → 填具体内容 → 调语气。
>
> 本文件把旧 `C_语言积累/02_小作文功能句.md` 与根目录 `考研英语一小作文体系_v1.0.md` 合并成一套：**格式 + P1/P2/P3 + 功能句**。不背整篇范文。

---

## 0. 怎么用

```text
P1 Purpose   我为什么写
P2 Content   我要具体说什么
P3 Closure   怎样自然结束
```

- 篇幅：P1 约 20%，P2 约 60%，P3 约 20%。开头两句内，主体永远是重点。
- 一封信通常 **1 个主功能 + 1–2 个辅助功能**（如 Invite + Explain）。
- 题面每个显性要求都要有对应句；漏一项比语言平淡严重。
- 语域跟着收件人走：给朋友用正式句，反而奇怪。
- 组装顺序：细节 → 查本文件的功能句 → 填题面内容 → 检查漏项。

## 1. 基本格式与语域

### Letter / Formal Email（正式）

```text
Dear Mr. Wang, / Dear Professor Smith, / Dear Sir or Madam,

[P1]

[P2]

[P3]

Yours sincerely,          （知道姓名）
Yours faithfully,         （Dear Sir or Madam）
Li Ming
```

用于教授、老师、机构、不熟悉的收件人；application / complaint / formal request。

### Friendly Email（友好）

```text
Dear Paul, / Hi Paul,

[P1]

[P2]

[P3]

Best wishes, / Best,
Li Ming
```

用于朋友、同学、熟悉的同龄人。

### Notice（通知）

```text
NOTICE

[正文：目的 → 时间地点对象 → 具体安排 / 要求 → 截止 / 联系方式]

Students' Union
[Date]
```

无称呼、无 Yours sincerely；第一句直接宣布事项。

### Memo（备忘录）

```text
MEMO

To: ____
From: ____
Date: ____
Subject: ____

[正文：目的 → 事项 → 行动要求]
```

无称呼、无落款；身份关系由头部字段建立。

### 语域速查

| 语域 | 对象 | 称呼 | 落款 | 特征 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formal | 教授 / 机构 / 陌生人 | Dear Professor … / Dear Sir or Madam | Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully | 完整句、少情绪、不堆口语 |
| Neutral | 一般事务、国际学生 | Dear Mr./Ms. + 姓 | Yours sincerely / Best regards | 稳定通用 |
| Friendly | 朋友 / 同学 | Dear / Hi + 名 | Best wishes / Best | 自然，可口语一些 |

- Formal 与 Friendly 是**关系距离**差异，不是水平高低。
- 不叠加礼貌：`I was wondering if you could possibly maybe...` 是过度礼貌。

## 2. P1 Purpose

> 每个功能留最稳的 2–4 条。未标语域的是中性（多数场合可用）。

### 通用开头

- I'm writing regarding ...
- I wanted to get in touch about ...
- My name is ..., and I'm a ... at ...（第一次联系陌生人）

### Invite（邀请）

- I'd like to invite you to ...
- Would you like to join us for ...?
- We'd be delighted if you could join us for ...
- Formal: It would be a pleasure to have you with us at ...

### Recommend（推荐）

- I'd recommend ...
- If you're looking for ..., ... would be a good choice.
- I'd especially recommend ... because ...
- Formal: I believe ... would be particularly suitable for ...

### Advise（建议）

- You might want to ...
- Perhaps you could ...
- It may be worth ...ing.
- I'd suggest ...ing.

### Request（请求）

- Could you please ...?
- I was wondering if you could ...
- I'd appreciate it if you could ...
- Formal: Would it be possible for you to ...?

> 礼貌程度按"关系距离 + 请求负担"选一档，不叠加。

### Apologize（道歉）

- I'm sorry for ... / I'm sorry that ...
- I apologize for ...
- I owe you an apology for ...
- Formal: Please accept my apologies for ...

### Thank（感谢）

- Thank you for ...
- I really appreciate ...
- It was very kind of you to ...
- I'm especially grateful that you ...

### Inform（告知 / 介绍）

- I'm writing to let you know that ...
- I wanted to let you know that ...
- I'm pleased to let you know that ... / I'm sorry to let you know that ...
- Formal: Please note that ...

### Reply（回复）

- Thank you for your message about ...
- It was great to hear from you.
- Regarding your question about ..., ...
- As for ..., ...（一个问题，一个回答）

### Complain（投诉）

- I'm writing about a problem with ...
- Unfortunately, ... did not ... as expected.
- I was disappointed to find that ...
- Formal: I would like to raise a concern about ...

### Apply（申请）

- I'm writing to apply for ...
- I would like to apply for ...
- With a background in ..., I believe I would be well suited to ...
- I would welcome the opportunity to ...

## 3. P2 Content

> P2 只解决"具体信息怎么说清楚"。内容由题目给出，不在这里发明。

### 时间 / 地点

- The event will be held at ... on ...
- It will take place from ... to ... in ...
- The meeting point is ...
- Participants are expected to arrive by ...

### 安排 / 活动

- During the event, you will have the opportunity to ...
- The event will include ... and ...
- Participants will be divided into ... groups.
- Further details will be provided after registration.

### 原因 / 说明

- The reason is that ...
- The main reason I ... is that ...
- Unfortunately, ..., which made it impossible for me to ...
- This was mainly due to ...

### 建议内容

- I suggest that you ..., which will help you ...
- It would be useful to ... so that you can ...
- One option would be to ...
- A practical way to ... would be to ...

### 请求内容

- I would appreciate it if you could ...
- Would you be able to ... by ...?
- If possible, could you also ...?
- Could you please look into this and let me know ...?

### 补充细节（匹配 / 影响 / 回复要点）

- Given your interest in ..., I believe ... would be an excellent choice for you.
- What I appreciated most was ... It helped me ...
- This will give you a chance to ...
- As for ..., ... Regarding ..., ...

## 3A. 高频场景词

### A. 邀请 / 通知 / 活动

- academic conference; lecture; seminar
- competition / contest; research and innovation competition; entrepreneurship training camp
- training session; mentor guidance; team project
- volunteer activity; cultural activity; campus activity
- opening ceremony; graduation ceremony
- schedule / arrangements; venue; main content; how to register / registration details

### B. 咨询 / 求助

- ask about the course / the event arrangements / the fee
- ask about accommodation / transport
- further information; ask for advice; ask for help / seek assistance
- confirm the details; contact information

### C. 推荐 / 介绍 / 文化交流

- recommend a book / a film / a tourist attraction
- introduce Chinese culture; traditional culture; intangible cultural heritage
- lion dance; experience traditional culture; watch a live performance
- take part in a hands-on workshop; visit a museum; cultural exchange

### D. 道歉 / 解决问题

- take ... by mistake; lose ...; damage ...
- be late; be absent; cause inconvenience
- explain the reason; take responsibility
- return ... / replace ...; compensate someone for ... / compensation
- a solution; apologize again

### E. 祝贺 / 感谢 / 人际沟通

- win first prize; achieve success; make progress; research achievements
- congratulate someone on ...; express congratulations
- express gratitude; thank someone for their help; thank someone for the invitation
- share one's experience; introduce the competition; keep in touch

### F. 申请 / 建议 / 校园生活

- apply for a position; apply to be a volunteer
- personal strengths; relevant experience; relevant skills / ability to do the job
- offer suggestions; improve the service
- learning efficiency; campus environment; library services; club activities; time management

## 4. P3 Closure

> 只保留真正不同的结束功能；一句话即可。

### 期待回复

- I look forward to hearing from you.
- Please let me know if you need any further information.

### 感谢

- Thank you for your time and consideration.
- I would appreciate your help.
- Please accept my sincere thanks once again.

### 友好

- Hope to see you soon.
- Feel free to let me know if you have any other questions.
- I hope you will enjoy it as much as I do.

### 道歉 / 理解

- Thank you for your understanding.
- Please accept my sincere apologies once again.

### 建议 / 告知

- I hope these suggestions will be helpful.
- I hope this information will be helpful.

### 投诉 / 请求解决

- I would appreciate it if this could be resolved soon.

### 邀请确认 / 祝愿

- I sincerely hope you will be able to join us.
- Please let me know if you are available.
- We look forward to your participation.

## 5. Notice

> 公共语域：impersonal + concise + action-oriented。少寒暄、多事实与动作。

**信息槽顺序：** What → Why → When/Where → Who → What to do → Deadline / Contact。

### 宣布事项

- A ... will be held on ...
- There will be a ... on ...
- The ... will take place on ...

### 目的

- The event aims to ...
- The purpose of the event is to ...

### 时间 / 地点

- The event will be held at ... on ...
- It will take place from ... to ... in ...

### 对象 / 资格

- The event is open to all students.
- Students interested in ... are encouraged to attend.
- No previous experience is required.

### 要求 / 报名

- Please register by ...
- Those interested should register by ...
- Participants are required to ...
- Please bring ... with you.

### 截止

- The deadline for registration is ...
- Please register no later than ...
- Places are limited, so early registration is recommended.

### 提醒 / 禁止

- Please note that ...
- Participants are not allowed to ...

### 联系 / 收尾

- For further information, please contact ...
- We look forward to your participation.

## 6. Memo

> 内部行政语域：第一句直接进入事务，不寒暄。

### 目的

- This memo is to inform you that ...
- This memo outlines ...
- The purpose of this memo is to ...

### 进展 / 变更

- So far, we have completed ...
- The schedule has been revised as follows.
- There has been a change to ...

### 任务 / 期限

- Please ... by ...
- You will be responsible for ...
- Please submit ... to ... by ...

### 反馈

- Please send your comments by ...
- I would appreciate your feedback on ...

### 收尾

- Please let me know if you have any questions.
- Thank you for your cooperation.

## 7. 常见组合

```text
邀请 = Invite + 时间地点 + 活动 + 希望参加 + Closure
推荐 = Recommend + 特点 + 匹配理由 + Closure
建议 = Advise + 2–3 条建议 + 简短理由 + Closure
请求 = Request + 具体内容 + 原因 / 期限 + Closure
道歉 = Apologize + 原因 + 补救 + Closure
感谢 = Thank + 具体帮助 + 作用 + Closure
投诉 = Problem + Impact + 期望解决 + Closure
回复 = 回应 + 逐项回答 + Closure
申请 = Apply + 背景 / 资格 + 意愿 + Closure
通知 = Purpose + Time/Place + Requirements + Deadline / Contact
```

## 8. 使用纪律

- 题面每个显性要求都要有对应句；先数要求，再写。
- 一封信一个主功能；辅助功能不超过两个。
- 不背整篇范文；用"格式 + P1/P2/P3 + 功能句"现场组装。
- Notice 不写 `Dear...`、不写 `Yours sincerely`；Memo 不写称呼和落款，头部字段优先。
- 正式邮件不堆口语和情绪；朋友邮件不必硬套正式句。
- 不用老套句：`I am writing this letter for the purpose of...` / `It is universally acknowledged that...` / `last but not least` / `...at your earliest convenience`。
- 大作文的通用功能句不搬进小作文；小作文也不借用大作文的论证语域。
- 具体 > 漂亮：时间、地点、活动、对象、截止日期写清楚，比换高级词重要。
- 回复题按对方提问顺序逐项回答，不漏问。
