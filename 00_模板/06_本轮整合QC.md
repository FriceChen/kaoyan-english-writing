The number of air conditioners rose steadily from 75.2 in 2014 to 145.9 in 2023, and there was a sustained increase in washing machines, from 83.7 to 98.2. Refrigerators showed a similar pattern, climbing from 85.5 to 103.4 over the same period.

Several factors may help explain this pattern. Rising incomes have made household appliances more affordable for ordinary families. In addition, product upgrades—such as energy-saving and smart features—have encouraged families to replace older models rather than simply keep one appliance for decades.

Overall, this trend reflects improved living standards rather than simple consumption. As ownership approaches saturation, what matters next is not how many appliances families own, but how well these products serve everyday needs—quietly, safely and efficiently.

### 试装 3｜2023 龙舟（纯图画，约 168 词，新写）

As is depicted above, the picture is set against the background of a dragon boat race: several rowers, moving in perfect rhythm, are paddling the long boat forward, while crowds on the bank cheer for their team. It is easy to catch a feeling of excitement from their conduct. Though superficially simple, this painting harbors a profound meaning.

The picture draws attention to the value of traditional culture in everyday life. In the course of such folk activities, people stand a good chance of strengthening emotional ties, not to mention the fact that they can ease their loneliness. Moreover, traditions kept alive through practice give younger generations a direct experience of the culture they inherit.

Therefore, passing on traditional culture does not mean keeping it in a museum; it means keeping it on the water, on the stage and in daily life. What matters most is that communities continue to take part, so that the tradition stays alive rather than merely recorded.
