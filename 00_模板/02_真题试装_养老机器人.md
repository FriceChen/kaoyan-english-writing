# 真题试装：2026英语一·养老机器人

> 用途：展示“模板→现有素材→现有表达”的一次完整调用。**以下英文是新写的练习样例，不是商业模板原文或官方范文。**

## A. 破题卡（写前用中文确认）

- **类型**：两个静态图表。
- **事实**：39.3%完全接受、32.9%部分接受、27.8%不接受；安全为受访者最常提及的顾虑（46.3%），随后为价格（24.9%）和便利（10.7%）。
- **中心**：机器人照护受到一定程度的认可，但安全和成本顾虑不容忽视。
- **机制**：辅助完成重复性照护任务 → 可能减轻部分负担；安全/价格顾虑 → 影响信任与采用。
- **结论**：机器人应当辅助而非完全代替人的照护；提高安全性与易用性。

## B. 三段完整样例（约175词）

The two charts present the results of a survey on attitudes towards elderly-care robots. In the first chart, 39.3% of respondents fully accept such robots, while 32.9% partly accept them and 27.8% do not. The second chart shows that safety is the most frequently cited concern (46.3%), followed by price (24.9%) and convenience (10.7%).

The figures suggest that interest in robotic care is accompanied by considerable caution. On the one hand, robots may help older adults with routine tasks and ease some of the pressure on family caregivers. On the other hand, people may hesitate to rely on these devices if they are expensive or difficult to trust. Safety concerns, in particular, can matter greatly when a product is used in everyday care. Therefore, wider acceptance depends not only on what robots can do, but also on how reliably and affordably they can do it.

In my view, elderly-care robots should complement rather than replace human care. Improving product safety and making the devices easier to use would help more families benefit from this technology.

## C. 这个例子调用了什么？

- **模板**：第一段“静态数据并列”，第二段“益处＋顾虑”，第三段“评价＋条件”。
- **素材**：照护负担、日常辅助任务、价格、安全、信任。
- **思路**：辅助与替代的区别；技术可及不等于有效使用。
- **表达**：respondents, cited concern, on the one hand/on the other hand, depend on, complement rather than replace。

## D. 为什么比机械套入“流行＋两原因”更合适？

题面没有说明该技术已大量上市或购买，不能把接受态度直接视为“普及”。第二张图不仅提供原因的候选线索，也直接提示“实际采用的障碍”，应当在讨论中得到回应。
