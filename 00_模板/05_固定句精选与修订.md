# 05 固定句精选与修订（L1 可用层）

> **性质：** 经审查的"商业模板有效语言收录表"，按**写作功能**组织。每条 = 推荐句 + 中文意思 + 适用条件 + 原文 ID。带 ⚠ 的条目说明原句问题与改写理由；详细原句见 `04_购买模板固定语言总索引.md`（L0）。
> **分工边界：** 句子级语言以 `03_表达`（Position/Shared/Figure/Topic）为规范参考；本文件收的是商业模板中**值得复现的固定句及其修订版**。句子只在真实写作采用后才进 `09_人语料库`。

## Ⅰ 首段

### Ⅰ-1 静态图（单图，调查/占比类）
- **推荐：** The chart shows the results of a survey on ____. Among the options, ____ was the most frequently selected, at __%, followed by ____ (__%) and ____ (__%).
- 中文：图表展示了关于____的调查结果。在所有选项中，____被选最多，占__%……
- 条件：调查/选项类静态图。⚠ 原句 `To ascertain..., the chart aggregated...` 虚构调查过程；`was the largest` 把选项占比混成"大小"（PUR-04-P10-003）。
- 备注：项目名英文化 = 对象+比例名词化（the proportion of students planning to...，PUR-04-P14-013）；时态用一般过去时（PUR-04-P11-008）；选项<4 删尾项、>4 末项可省（PUR-04-P11-004）。

### Ⅰ-2 动态图（时间变化类）
- **推荐：** The chart illustrates changes in ____ between YEAR and YEAR. ____ rose/fell steadily from __ to __; there was a sustained increase/decrease in ____, from __ to __; and ____ continued to expand/decline, from __ to __.
- 中文：图表展示了____在两年间的变化……
- 条件：两个及以上年份。⚠ 原句 `sustainable growth` 误用（sustainable=可持续；此处应为 sustained，PUR-04-P13-012）；`A stable increase ... was recorded` 被动别扭（PUR-04-P14-015）。
- 变体：**____ remained essentially unchanged (__%).**（不变趋势，PUR-04-P15-016）。

### Ⅰ-3 双图表（静+静）
- **推荐：** The two charts present the results of a survey on ____. In the first chart, ____ was the most frequently selected, at __%, followed by ____ (__%). The second chart shows that ____ was the most commonly cited ____ (__%), followed by ____ (__%).
- 条件：两张静态调查图（如 2026 养老机器人）。⚠ 同 Ⅰ-1 修订（PUR-04-P19-021）。

### Ⅰ-4 双图表（动+动）
- **推荐：** The two charts illustrate changes in ____. In the first chart, ____ rose/fell steadily from...; in the second chart, ____ saw a sustained increase/decrease from...
- 条件：两张时间图。⚠ 原句把图表称作 picture（PUR-04-P21-023），按图型用 chart/graph。

### Ⅰ-5 双图表（静+动）
- **推荐：** The chart and the graph present ____. The chart shows that ____ was the most frequently selected, at __%...; the graph shows ____ rising/falling steadily from __ to __.
- 条件：一静一动（PUR-04-P22-024）。

### Ⅰ-6 图画（四句框架）
1. 引入：**As is depicted above, ...** / **The picture is set against the background of ____.** / 双图 **The two pictures are set against the background of ____.** / 兜底 **...the background of a philosophical story.**（PUR-04-P23~24）
2. 动作：**Before our eyes stands/sits/walks ____, doing/done ____.**（次要动作族：sit/stand/walk/run/lie/jump/float/curl up；主动 doing、被动 done，PUR-04-P24~25）
3. 情绪：**It is easy to catch a feeling of ____ from his/her/their conduct.**（pleasure/anger/sorrow/puzzlement/embarrassment/indifference/fatigue/confidence，PUR-04-P25）
4. 分类收尾：一图一主角 **Though superficially simple, this painting harbors a profound meaning.**；一图两主角 **At this very moment, ____ is doing ____.**；两图 **In the second picture, ____.**（PUR-04-P25）

### Ⅰ-7 图画＋动态图
- **推荐：** The two pictures are set against the background of ____. In the first picture stands ____, doing ____. It is easy to catch a feeling of ____ from ____. In the second picture, the number of ____ rose steadily from __ in __ to __ in __.（PUR-04-P27-042）

### Ⅰ-8 图画＋静态图
- **推荐：** 同上首两句 + The second picture showed that, of all the options, ____ was the most frequently selected, at __%.（PUR-04-P28-043，⚠ 同 Ⅰ-1 修订）

### Ⅰ-9 文字材料
- **推荐：** Never in recent memory has there been so much discussion about whether ____. This is a matter that touches everyone. As far as I'm concerned, ____ is of great significance, which coincides with the author.
- 条件：仅完全同意作者时用尾句；部分同意改 **While I share the author's concern about ____, I believe ____.**（PUR-04-P29-045）

## Ⅱ 主体

### Ⅱ-0 段落路径选择（先于一切句子）
- 增长/选择/行为类图表 → 原因型；品质/设施/行为价值 → 意义型；接受与顾虑、便利与风险、批判性图画 → 关系与条件型（见 `00_考场执行卡`）。
- ⚠ **红线（PUR-05-P33-002）**：调查的"关注因素/使用目的/接受度"不等于"该事物流行"；主题句按图表实际关系写。

### Ⅱ-1 原因·媒体
- **Compared to the past, widespread publicity for ____ has not only sparked considerable interest among people, but also motivated them to ____.**
- 条件：宣传媒体与现象的因果需常识支持；不可凭空归因（如无媒体信息不得写广告导致购买）。（PUR-05-P35-004）

### Ⅱ-2 原因·眼界
- **Few would disagree that ____ will enable people both to sample a different way of life and to broaden their horizons, which underlies the popularity of ____.**
- 条件：涉新体验、异文化、旅游展览类。（PUR-05-P36-005）

### Ⅱ-3 原因·收入与省钱
- ⚠ 修订：原句 `permit more residents to well afford to` 搭配错误（well afford，PUR-05-P38-006）。
- **推荐：** Rising personal incomes have made it easier for more people to afford ____.（保留强调句变体：It is rising incomes that enable ...）
- 变体（省钱）：**Ordinarily, it will work out cheaper to ____, and this factor attracts more and more consumers desirous of saving money.**（PUR-05-P41-010）

### Ⅱ-4 原因·赚钱与缺钱
- **It is no secret that thousands of people, especially the young, are lured into ____ by the prospect of better jobs and more money. Indeed, it's human nature to desire a better life.**（PUR-05-P39-007）
- 变体（缺钱）：**It is no secret that lack of money is one of the reasons why ____.**（PUR-05-P41-009）

### Ⅱ-5 原因·快乐
- **Notable among these reasons is the fact that ____ now constitutes such a source of delight that it can bring pleasure to people, especially the younger generation, and lift their spirits.**
- 条件：休闲、娱乐、社交类现象。（PUR-05-P42-011）

### Ⅱ-6 原因·健康与压力
- **In order to promote physical and emotional well-being, numerous people have developed a habit of ____.**（PUR-05-P43-012）
- 变体（压力驱动）：**Word has it that the pressures which stem from work, study and family have overwhelmed more and more adults, who therefore ____.**（PUR-05-P44-013）
- 变体（减压）：**____, the antidote to stress, can free people from a competitive existence, as well as enable them to restore faith in life.**（PUR-05-P45-014）

### Ⅱ-7 原因·政策
- **Were it not for the fact that the policy on ____ has been widely advocated in recent years, we would fail to witness the popularity of ____.**
- 条件：**仅当确有可指认的政策背景**（如垃圾分类、全民健身）；政策事实不明时改 `Public investment/support may have contributed to ...`。（PUR-05-P46-015；总纲红线 3）

### Ⅱ-8 原因·社交（正/反）
- 正：**In the course of ____, people stand a good chance of winning more friends / cementing emotional ties, not to mention the fact that they can ease their loneliness.**（PUR-05-P47-016）
- 反：**Handling interpersonal relationships really drains the vast majority of people, so ____ is only to be expected.**（PUR-05-P48-017）

### Ⅱ-9 原因·竞争（修订）与负面批评
- ⚠ 修订：原句 `A recent study suggests that ...` 虚构研究来源（PUR-05-P50-019）。
- **推荐：** One possible explanation is that the more competitive society becomes, the more likely people are to ____.
- 负面批评：**Although the economy has grown rapidly in recent years, there follows a series of problems, which is best exemplified by the fact that ____.**（PUR-05-P40-008）

### Ⅱ-10 原因·科技与时间
- **Thanks to technological advances, ____, which represents the latest in technology, has been designed to be powerful as well as intelligent, prompting more people to ____.**（PUR-05-P49-018）
- 变体（省时间）：**Leading a busy and hectic life, people become increasingly willing to replace ____, which wastes time and energy, with ____, whose advantage is time-saving.**（PUR-05-P51-020）

### Ⅱ-11 重要性引出（图画/画表/文字）
- 积极寓意图：**The principal purpose of this picture is to highlight the significance of ____.**（⚠ 原句 preach 仅适用积极寓意；消极/讽刺图改 **The picture draws attention to ...** / **The contrast points to ...**，PUR-05-P53-023~025）
- 变体：**What the painter intends to advocate is _____.**

### Ⅱ-12 论证·个人成长
- 充实快乐（⚠ inevitably 绝对化已改，PUR-05-P54-026）：**Although ____ does not necessarily revolutionize our lifestyle, it can make daily life more fulfilled and satisfying.**
- 正能量：**Where there is an individual with the merit of ____ in a group, there is positive energy spreading among all members, though not always consciously.**（PUR-05-P54-027）
- 竞争力（⚠ bestowed upon 已改，PUR-05-P54-028）：**Considering that people from all walks of life wish to sharpen their competitive edges, it is sensible for them to ____, which will give them more freedom and choices later on.**
- 进步（⚠ no question about eventual success 绝对化已改，PUR-05-P55-029）：**Once a person is on the road to ____, steady progress is likely to follow.**
- 认可：**The better one performs in ____, the more respect he or she will gain in interpersonal communication.**（PUR-05-P55-031）

### Ⅱ-13 论证·家庭 / 社会文化
- 家庭：**Few, if any, would dispute that it pays to ____ because of the tremendous benefit to a healthy relationship among family members.**（PUR-05-P55-030）
- 眼界（⚠ ineligible to evade 搭配错误已改，PUR-05-P56-033）：**In an ever-changing information age, few of us can avoid ____, which enables us to sample a different way of life and broaden our horizons.**
- 身心健康：**Closely associated with individual health, ____ will enable people not only to feel well both physically and mentally, but also to cultivate a positive approach to life.**（PUR-05-P56-034）

### Ⅱ-14 论证·环境
- 类比：**It may be stating the obvious, but nature is to human beings what a mother is to her children, and thus it is wise to ____ so as to protect the natural environment.**（PUR-05-P57-036）
- 造福：**Anyone wishing to appreciate the beauties of nature should establish the habit of ____, which is beneficial to the future of all humankind.**（PUR-05-P57-037）

### Ⅱ-15 由负转正（使用规则）
- 消极图画**先提炼积极主题词**再套重要性语料：手机聚会→人际交流；污染→环境保护；不自信→自信；盲目崇拜→理性追星；不孝→孝顺；溺爱→家庭教育。（PUR-05-P58-039）
- 边界：提炼后的主题词必须仍与画面事实一致（事实/解释分离，见 `01_看材料`）。

## Ⅲ 结尾

### Ⅲ-1 评价
- **Overall, ____ can be valuable because ____. What matters most is ____.**

### Ⅲ-2 条件
- **The benefits of ____ will be greater if ____.**
- 图表收尾（⚠ 原句 `will ... ultimately change their lifestyle` 无根据预测已改，PUR-06-P60-001）：**In brief, a combination of factors may help explain ____. If current conditions persist, it will likely continue to attract attention.**

### Ⅲ-3 建议
- **To make better use of ____, ____ should ____.**
- ⚠ 原句 `the mass media are supposed to motivate the public to recognize the seriousness of ____` 把任何对象当"严重问题"且媒体万金油（PUR-06-P60-002）；按主题给具体措施，如 Local authorities should maintain these facilities properly.
